The problems we fix

Most small business websites are not broken. They just quietly lose people. These are the issues we see most often.

Hard to use on a phone

Text too small, buttons too close together, menus that do not open. Most visitors arrive on mobile.

Slow to load

Heavy images and clutter make people leave before the page appears.

No clear next step

Visitors cannot quickly see how to call, book, buy or get a quote.

Hard to find on Google

Missing page titles, descriptions and headings give search engines little to work with.

Broken or outdated bits

Dead links, old opening hours, missing contact details and forms that never arrive.

Copy that does not sell

Vague wording that says what you are, not why a customer should choose you.

Content that gets used once

A great podcast, video or webinar goes out once, then disappears.

No time to post

Social and newsletter content happens in bursts, then stops for weeks.
